Job Title: NLP / AI Engineer
Company: Cohere
Location: Toronto, Canada (Remote-friendly)
Department: Research & Engineering

About the Role:
Cohere is building world-class large language models and we are looking for a talented NLP / AI Engineer who is passionate about language AI. You will work at the intersection of research and engineering to build, fine-tune, and deploy NLP models that power our core products — including text generation, classification, embeddings, and semantic search.

Responsibilities:
- Research, implement, and optimize NLP models including transformers, fine-tuning LLMs (GPT, BERT, T5, LLaMA), and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems.
- Design and build embeddings and semantic search pipelines using vector databases (Pinecone, Chroma, Weaviate).
- Develop data preprocessing, annotation pipelines, and datasets for supervised and self-supervised training.
- Evaluate model quality using standard NLP benchmarks (GLUE, SuperGLUE, MMLU) and custom evaluations.
- Work with the infrastructure team to optimize model inference (quantization, ONNX, TensorRT).
- Stay current with state-of-the-art NLP research and implement key papers into production systems.
- Write clean, efficient code and contribute to internal libraries and tooling.

Required Qualifications:
- Bachelor's or Master's/PhD in Computer Science, NLP, Computational Linguistics, or related field.
- 3+ years of experience in NLP or machine learning engineering.
- Deep understanding of transformer architectures (BERT, GPT, T5, LLaMA, Mistral).
- Proficiency in Python with hands-on experience using HuggingFace Transformers, PyTorch.
- Practical experience fine-tuning pre-trained language models (LoRA, PEFT, instruction tuning).
- Strong knowledge of NLP tasks: text classification, named entity recognition, question answering, summarization.
- Experience building and querying vector databases for semantic search.
- Familiarity with MLflow or similar experiment tracking tools.
- Solid SQL skills and experience with data processing at scale.

Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience with RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback) or DPO alignment techniques.
- Contributions to NLP open-source projects or published research papers.
- Experience with multilingual NLP and cross-lingual transfer.
- Familiarity with LangChain or LlamaIndex for building LLM-powered applications.
- Knowledge of efficient inference techniques: quantization (GPTQ, AWQ), speculative decoding.

Technologies Stack:
- Languages: Python (primary), C++ (optional)
- NLP/ML: HuggingFace Transformers, PyTorch, scikit-learn, spaCy
- Vector DBs: Pinecone, Chroma, Weaviate
- Experiment Tracking: MLflow, Weights & Biases
- Infrastructure: Docker, Kubernetes, AWS/GCP
- Data: Apache Spark, Airflow, SQL, pandas, numpy
